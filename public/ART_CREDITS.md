# Artwork credits

**The Starry Night (1889)** — Vincent van Gogh, oil on canvas, Museum of Modern Art (New York).

The bundled `starry-night.webp` is an optimized derivative of a faithful digital reproduction of this public-domain painting. Public-domain source reference: [Wikimedia Commons — VanGogh-starry night.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:VanGogh-starry_night.jpg). The painting is public domain due to the artist's death in 1890. This site includes the image only for an educational interactive painting demonstration.

All other Rubik card and miniature city geometry is original programmatic design for Digital Creators. No image or 3D model has been copied from PeachWeb.
